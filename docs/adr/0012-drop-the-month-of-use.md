# ADR 0012: Drop the month of personal use

## Status
Accepted.

## Context
M6 ended with a month of personal use, and the PRD listed it as an MVP success criterion. Everything else in M0 to M6 is built. The month held the MVP open while post-MVP work went ahead anyway. The project has one developer, who is also the only user and uses the app day to day regardless.

## Options
- Keep the month as M6's last item and an MVP success criterion
- Move it out of M6 as a separate check alongside post-MVP work
- Drop it

## Decision
Drop it. M6 is done, and the MVP is judged by the other two success criteria: rough notes to an approved draft in under ten minutes, and a ZIP round-trip with zero content loss.

## Consequences
- The MVP is complete once its milestones are built.
- Problems found in daily use are fixed as they come, with no separate review at the end of a month.
